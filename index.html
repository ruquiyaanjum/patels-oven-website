The maths behind 5.0

Google shows the average rounded to one decimal. With 9 reviews, a 5.0 is only possible if every single review is five stars:

  1. All 9 reviews give 5 stars45 ÷ 9 = 5.00 → 5.0
  2. Only one review gives 4 stars44 ÷ 9 = 4.89 → 4.9

So 5.0 from 9 reviews means 9 out of 9 customers gave five stars. Do not take our word for it: read them yourself.
