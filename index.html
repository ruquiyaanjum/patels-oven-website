Our Story

A new beginning in December 2025

Welcome to The Patel's Oven

Established in December 2025, The Patel's Oven is our new family bakery in Anekal, Bengaluru. We started with a simple dream: to create exceptional baked goods with clarity and purpose.

We specialize in three things we do best: Cupcakes, Cakes, and Cookies. By focusing on these specialties, we ensure every item we bake is perfected.

Our prices range from ₹50 for our delicious cupcakes to ₹3000+ for custom celebration cakes. We believe everyone should be able to enjoy quality baked goods, which is why we keep our prices affordable without compromising on quality.

Our Commitment to Quality

"We use only fresh, high-quality ingredients. No preservatives, no shortcuts. Every cupcake, cake, and cookie is baked fresh daily with the care and attention it deserves."
