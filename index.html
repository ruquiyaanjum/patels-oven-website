Inside every slice

Four steps,one dessert.

    The sponge

    A light, airy sponge, baked fresh in our oven.

  2. 02

    The soak

    Tres leches means "three milks". The sponge drinks them in, with the taste of rasmalai.

  3. 03

    The cream

    Malai cream, smoothed on and piped into rosettes by hand.

  4. 04

    The finish

    Chopped pistachio and dried rose petals, placed one by one.
